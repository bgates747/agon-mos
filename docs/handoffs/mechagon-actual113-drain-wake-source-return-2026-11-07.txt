Concrete seams

1. UART1 source versus drain. u1_step sets U1_TAIL, clears its source/callback ownership and calls source_done AFTER emitting the final bytes. It explicitly rejects the PRE-write TEMT as stale. That event returns RELEASED|RETRY. The SDK's source_done publishes source_released and then AGON_NETA_NOTIFY, and phase changes also notify after publication. The owner maps TAIL to demand3 and enables TX IRQ only for demand1; demand3 clears TX-empty enable. Consequently a source callback can wake the app while the last bytes are still physically draining. A foreground progress call at that instant can still return BUSY. The later TEMT transition has no dedicated armed TEMT IRQ in this composition. A later clean RX-first service sample must see TEMT before U1_TAIL -> IDLE/DRAINED; only then does the phase-change notification publish that newly observed fact.

2. UART1 CTS retry. While U1_SOURCE sees not-CTS, it returns RETRY without source release or a phase change. SDK reports demand2 and owner disables TX IRQ. PC3 is read as a GPIO STOP/CTS level, not an armed modem/port edge-wake path here. The existing owner tick, UART0 exit-tail or supported foreground progress can resample and restart TX when CTS is ready. The application must not pretend a pending hint tracks every CTS edge. Do not force notify on every unchanged service call: that can turn a legitimate wait into an immediate foreground service loop.

3. UART0 has its own two cases. The bounded-CTS derivative masks TXIER and sets uart0_tx_retry_pending on CTS blockage; uart0_async_retry re-arms only from the existing VBlank handler, after a ready PD_DR sample. GenericU1 cannot speed up that separate retry mechanism. At final UART0 fill the RFC ISR disables TXIER and calls render_rfc_callback before the FIFO's wire tail drains. Your callback sets render_rfc_done and pending; it does not prove TEMT. render_link_network_ready and transmit_progress still correctly require the later portC5bit6 observation. A tagged VDPlet reply can independently wake/reveal progress, but it is not guaranteed to be present at the source callback and remains a separate semantic condition.

4. WAIT1 works as designed. wake_app_begin clears/snapshots BEFORE the complete foreground pass. aw_wait under DI rechecks pending plus all four clock bytes, then uses adjacent EI/HALT. A callback or observed tick between snapshot and wait prevents stale sleep. A hardware-only TEMT transition which has not produced an IRQ/notification or a clock change is not visible to that test. HALT can wake for any admitted interrupt, including one that does not set pending; there is no promise that it waits until the next VBlank, nor a promise of a dedicated earlier wake. This distinction is a scheduling gap, not evidence the old predicate/HALT lost-wakeup bug returned.

Actual foreground policy matters too. transport_progress calls tx_progress first, can TAKE a ready reply into prefetched storage, and gates later submit on positive wire retirement plus render_link_network_ready. tx_cached_drain is only a previously positive per-source fact and retains current IDLE/no-fault/source requirements; it cannot be promoted before first positive drain. After an empty POLL, poll_after=now+1 deliberately defers another speculative POLL until a clock change. A STATE arriving at P4 just after that empty answer can wait for the next poll/service. After a data response, poll_after=now permits immediate continuation; queued SEND has priority over speculative POLL. These are conditional opportunities, not a guaranteed one-period-per-command tax.

Smallest supported useful-work route, advice for the consumer only

Keep the generic core, owner and WAIT1 leaf unchanged. At the existing before-sleep seam, execute at most one finite, resumable, genuinely needed host/game preparation quantum in disjoint storage, with interrupts enabled, then return to the complete ordinary progress/receive pass. Examples are bounded preparation of an already admitted view's next packet or independent bookkeeping; exact safe work belongs to MechAgon. Do not emit VDU/UART bytes, start graphics across the reciprocal NM gate, mutate an outstanding packet/client.input, assume a future STATE, or advance renderer shadow beyond its required completion endpoint. If a current STATE is already consumable, its decision/intent remains higher priority than optional preparation. When the finite useful backlog is exhausted, sleep through WAIT1; do not substitute dummy work or an unchanged-status retry loop to manufacture a polling cadence. Retain begin-before-all-checks, deadline/fault/owner validation and source/TEMT/reply/CLOSE lifetimes. This can reclaim useful work while waiting and may observe positive drain earlier at normal service boundaries, but cannot guarantee lower latency if there is no real work or if owner CTS retry still waits on VBlank.
