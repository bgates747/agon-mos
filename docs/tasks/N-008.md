link reaches the Console8 eZ80. This does not authorize repairing, erasing,
halting, dumping or programming its MOS. Normal Extender firmware on the P4
must be preserved. The Author subsequently directed that the recovery programmer
remain installed until MOS repair is finished; no automatic restoration is allowed.

The Author reports blue connected to Agon TDI, green to TCK, common ground,
separate USB power and both USB devices attached to Oryx. The P4 wire ends
are not independently confirmed. Follow Extender's maintained mos-recovery
protocol, using its P4 DevKit GPIO46/TCK and GPIO47/TDI assignments. Do not
infer physical Console8 connector positions from schematic numbering alone.

## Observed outcome — 2026-10-28

The complete 16 MiB incoming P4 flash was read, independently verified against
the physical device and copied to Pi before replacement. The installed
programmer's complete readback matches the exact expected write/erase composition;
bytes outside [0, 598016) are unchanged. The programmer remains installed.

The initial capture opened after esptool reset and received no bytes. The
mainboard joined-control opener was not qualified for this P4 native USB
interface. After reviewing the maintained P4 host and reset implementation,
one deliberate P4-only reset was captured with ordinary PySerial already open.
This captures a successful programmer boot, then:

```text
Identity 1 product=FFFF revision=FF status=FF
RECOVERY REFUSED: ZDI identity gate failed
Target eZ80 was not deliberately halted.
```

Expected product/revision is 0007/AA. The gate correctly stopped before a second
identity or host arming. No serial data bytes, DUMP, RESTORE, target halt or
Agon erase/program were sent. No retry after the observed identity refusal.
The first empty capture likely missed startup/refusal, but its exact cause is
not established by the second capture. It is preserved as failed evidence.

The next dependency is an Author physical check with both boards powered off:
blue/TDI to P4 GPIO47 (EXT2 pin6), green/TCK to GPIO46 (EXT2 pin7), common ground,
and the actual Console8 connector markings. An all-ones read is consistent with

Do not infer Console8 physical header positions from differently numbered symbols.
