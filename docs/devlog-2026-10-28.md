The Author subsequently requested broad Internet research, particularly Jeroen
Venema (envenomator). No hardware was accessed during this research. Findings:

- [Jeroen's maintained recovery utility](https://github.com/AgonPlatform/agon-recovery)
  explicitly supports Console8 through an external ESP32 (at least4MB flash),
  three wires, serial1152008N1 and MOS-only option2. Onboard C8 VDP lacks the
  available GPIO for the simpler self-recovery method. A spare classic WROOM
  DevKit is a documented fallback that avoids the custom P4 port.
- [Build configuration](https://github.com/AgonPlatform/agon-recovery/blob/master/platformio.ini)
  pins esp32dev/Arduino2.0.16, not P4. The current bundled MOS changed to3.0 in
  [commit95d68464](https://github.com/AgonPlatform/agon-recovery/commit/95d68464afd049945e1dfd780c1c07621d565450).
  Payload selection must remain explicit; a ready binary is not automatically
  our selected stock3.0.2. The complete public issues/PR list contains only the
  build-version/CI PR; no matching intermittent-ID diagnosis was found there.
- [Unlinked upstream pull-up drawing](https://github.com/AgonPlatform/agon-recovery/blob/master/media/console8_zdi_pullup.png)
  and its Fritzing source show10k from each ZDI signal to target3V3. However,
  [Heber's C8Rev2 schematic, printed16](https://heber.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Heber_Agon_Console8_user_manual-2.pdf#page=18)
  already has R92/R93,10k, on TCK/TDI. Actual population is unverified. This
  drawing does not establish a missing-pullup fault on our board. Its wire
  colours are opposite our harness; use signal names.
- [Zilog PS0153, Table110](https://www.zilog.com/docs/ez80acclaim/PS0153.pdf)
  specifies TCK LOW during RESET selects OCI/JTAG; HIGH selects ZDI. A controlled
  reset must preserve the required idle states. This is a credible mechanism
  to investigate, not the established cause of the changed IDs.
- [Zilog TN0035](https://www.zilog.com/docs/ez80acclaim/appnotes/tn0035.pdf)
  recommends pull-ups, tristated idle drivers, short connections and a good
  ground return; it identifies edge glitches and rare ground loops as causes
  of debug communication trouble. Inference from C8R92/R93: a powered P4 driving
  HIGH can feed the off C8rail through those resistors. No voltage was measured;
  do not assume a clean target power-off while outputs remain driven.
- [Official MOS instructions](https://github.com/AgonPlatform/agon-mos#programming-the-ez80-directly)
  document an Acclaim Smart Cable as another route. A
  [Pi Pico programmer](https://github.com/dinoboards/ez80-for-rc/blob/main/programmer/readme.md)
  exists, but its author qualifies RCBus hardware rather than Console8; this
  would introduce another adaptation. No purchase or new deployment was made.
- [Official update guidance](https://agonplatform.github.io/agon-docs/Updating-Firmware/)
  already documents a completed countdown without automatic reset. That symptom
  alone never established interrupted flashing; our baud-calculation defect
  remains independently demonstrated off-device.
