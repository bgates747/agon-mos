## Stock startup until explicit transport activation

The Author proposes preserving a proven stock boot/transport, activating the
experimental sender only by explicit CLI/application request, and forgetting
activation on reset so the updater remains reachable after an experiment fails.
Adopted this as a stronger design requirement, not a claim about delivered code.
Source review confirms that the closed candidate already routes UART0 IRQs to
tx_isr and ordinary status reads to experimental helpers. Session opt-in alone
does not isolate startup. The adopted native foundation's baud overflow also
predates session activation; a runtime gate alone would not have prevented it.

Recorded the contract in docs/uart0-async.md and its effect on the native tranche.
BO-001..BO-004 in the sole TODO cover baseline proof, isolated activation,
linked-image/reset fault controls, and separately authorized hardware acceptance.
N-006 now requires that work rather than treating the old arithmetic-only fix
as the next deployable package. Preserve normal drain/source lifetime and sole
RX ownership. Partial VDP packets may require a whole-board reset; corrupt flash
or unrelated startup defects remain outside a runtime enable flag's protection.

## Compiler policy clarified: upstream modifications versus original software

The Author explains the rationale: modifications/extensions of official ZDS II
firmware/software retain that compiler, keeping builds and changes familiar
for upstream review, testing and approval. For original software the preference
is free/open-source tools such as AgonDev. Standalone menu/test applications
are in that second category; permission for them is not a MOS compiler change.
